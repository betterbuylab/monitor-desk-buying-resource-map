# Buyer-fit checklist

- [ ] Is the panel primarily for competitive gaming, creative work, or mixed use?
- [ ] Does your GPU comfortably drive 4K, or is 1440p the practical ceiling?
- [ ] How deep is the desk, and will a 32-inch or ultrawide fit without neck strain?
- [ ] Do you need USB-C power delivery for a laptop dock workflow?
- [ ] Is HDR performance important, or is motion clarity the priority?
- [ ] Will the monitor sit beside a TV used for console gaming?
- [ ] Do you care more about color accuracy or high refresh for esports?
- [ ] Is budget flexibility more important than OLED on a desk?
