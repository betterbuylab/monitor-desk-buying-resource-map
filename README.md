## Buyer-fit questions

1. Is the panel primarily for competitive gaming, creative work, or mixed use?
2. Does your GPU comfortably drive 4K, or is 1440p the practical ceiling?
3. How deep is the desk, and will a 32-inch or ultrawide fit without neck strain?
4. Do you need USB-C power delivery for a laptop dock workflow?
5. Is HDR performance important, or is motion clarity the priority?
6. Will the monitor sit beside a TV used for console gaming?
7. Do you care more about color accuracy or high refresh for esports?
8. Is budget flexibility more important than OLED on a desk?
